About

I build things that run close to the metal. My work spans MCUs, FPGAs, and single-board computers — wherever software meets real hardware constraints and the datasheet becomes the ground truth.

I care about system reliability and low-power design — making devices that behave in the real world, not just on a bench under ideal conditions. If it breaks in the field, it doesn't count.

Right now I'm going deep into FPGA design — writing RTL, thinking in clock cycles, building systems where timing is everything and there's no OS to save you.
